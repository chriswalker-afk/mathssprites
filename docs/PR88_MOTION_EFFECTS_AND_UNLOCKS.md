# PR88 — Cosmetic movement, effects and reward celebrations

Adds two movement styles (Springy bounce, Magical levitation) and four effects (Stardust footsteps, Golden landing sparkles, Celestial particle trail, Constellation aura) using the existing catalogue, saved loadout and Atelier. 24 of 29 reward designs are now implemented. Movement and effects combine with all existing appearance slots. The native idle animation and shadow use bounded deterministic keyframes; baskets keep their existing bob. Effects use one pointer-transparent decorative layer within the whole sprite art envelope, with no new tokens or physics. Fraction fragments remain exact and undecorated; whole actors share the new cosmetic layer. Reduced motion shows a resting sprite with still effect artwork. The motion study contains all 27 comparison cards. Fraction presentation revision 88.

Campaign completion alone queues newly earned rewards. Ordinary replays announce at the completed final panel (including Show final working), Apprentice after the verified physical replay, fraction guided/independent at the final review panel and fraction Apprentice after playback. Closing/skipping a review acknowledges queued rewards without blocking the next activity. The notice is nonmodal, temporary, has no automatic equipment or navigation, does not capture focus or clicks, and is still under reduced motion. Existing rewards migrated on startup, repeated completions, previews and free play produce no celebration. No new stars, reward thresholds or save schema changes.

QA includes catalogue ownership, preview isolation, combinations, reduced motion, distinct effects, native actor markup, queue order/deduplication, main and fraction review timing.
