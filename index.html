AN APPRENTICE'S GUIDE

How to play

A dark sprite is an unknown value. Each brass weight counts as one. Leave one sprite opposite only weights.

  1. Choose an action and the objects to change.
  2. Balance by making equivalent changes to both rooms.
  3. Reveal the algebra behind your moves.
How do the three stages work?

Guided Balance: the hand repeats your chosen action in both rooms.

Independent Balance: you apply a matching move in each room yourself.

Algebra Apprentice: choose or type the operation in the maths editor. It calculates the next equation, then replays your steps with sprites.

What if my experiment goes wrong?

In the first two stages, watch what happens when a move makes fractions, overfills a room or leaves the scale uneven. The hand explains the problem and undoes the attempt. Your valid moves are kept.

The Inventor's Desk lets you build your own positive whole-number or fractional equations. Free play does not change your levels or stars.
